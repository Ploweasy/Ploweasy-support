PlowEasy Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

Introduction

PlowEasy ("we," "our," or "us") operates the PlowEasy Pro and PlowEasy Tracker mobile applications. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Services.

Information We Collect

PlowEasy Pro (Operator App)

Account Information: Name, email, phone number, business name, payment information (via Stripe)

Location Data: Real-time GPS coordinates only when you actively start a route. You control when location sharing is on/off.

Customer Information: Names, addresses, phone numbers, service history, optional photos

PlowEasy Tracker (Customer App)

Account Information: Operator code, optional email

No Location Data: The customer app does NOT collect or track your location. You only see your operator's location.

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide real-time GPS tracking
  • Send automatic notifications
  • Manage client information
  • Process subscription payments
  • Improve app features
  • Provide customer support

We Do NOT Sell Your Data

We DO NOT sell your personal information to anyone.

We share information only with:

  • Your customers/operator - Service-related information
  • Firebase (Google) - Data storage
  • Stripe - Payment processing (operators only)
  • Apple - Push notifications

Data Security

  • Industry-standard encryption
  • Secure authentication
  • Regular security updates
  • PCI-compliant payment processing

Your Rights

  • Access: View and update your information
  • Delete: Delete your account anytime
  • Control: Turn location sharing on/off
  • Notifications: Control which notifications you receive

Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Data retained while active
  • Deleted accounts: Data deleted within 30 days
  • Location history: Automatically deleted after 90 days

Children's Privacy

Our Services are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will notify you via email or in-app notification of any material changes.
