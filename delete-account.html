Delete Your PlowEasy Account

PlowEasy Pro and PlowEasy Tracker, by Harry Fay

You can ask us to delete your PlowEasy account and all of the data that goes with it at any time. This applies to the PlowEasy Pro operator app and the PlowEasy Tracker customer app, on both iPhone and Android.

How to request deletion

  1. Email PlowEasy2025@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my PlowEasy account".
  2. Send it from the email address you used in the app, and include your business name (operators) or your operator code (Tracker customers), so we can find your account.
  3. We'll reply to confirm, then delete your account and its data within 30 days. We'll email you when it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: business name, email address and phone number
  • Your clients and their properties, contact details and addresses
  • Your invoices and invoice PDFs
  • Your team: workers, invite codes and assigned routes
  • Live route and location data

What isn't deleted, and why

  • Data on your own phone (such as job notes, photos and route history) stays there until you delete the app.
  • Subscription and payment records are kept by Apple, Google, and Square (if you used it for card payments) under their own policies. Deleting your PlowEasy account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.
  • Location history is already removed automatically after 90 days.
Questions? Email PlowEasy2025@gmail.com. See our full Privacy Policy.
